Hello App Review team,

Thank you for the detailed questions on the 4.3 / 4.2.6 rejection of Settle In Peace. Answering each in full below.

1. WHAT THE APP DOES

Settle In Peace is the mobile client of settleinpeace.com — a debt-relief platform for US consumers. The app is account-based (the demo account in App Review Information opens the full product):

- Debt self-assessment: a guided questionnaire that captures unsecured debts — balances, creditors, rates, hardship context — and produces a summary of the user's situation.
- Dashboard: all logged debts and creditors in one place, with saved progress.
- Settlement tracking ("My Program"): enrollment status, trust-account progress, settlements in flight, and milestones for users in a settlement program.
- Creditor letters and documents: keep settlement correspondence organized.
- Learn: an educational library on debt relief, collections rights, and the settlement process, with per-article views.
- Calculators: payoff and savings estimates for comparing options.
- Coaching: guided content that walks users through the settlement journey step by step.
- Attorneys/providers directory: licensed professionals the user may choose to contact — referrals are made only with explicit user consent.
- Profile: account settings and in-app account deletion (Profile → Delete Account, backed by a live DELETE endpoint).

2. TARGET AUDIENCE

US adults carrying unsecured debt (credit cards, personal loans, medical bills) who are evaluating or participating in debt settlement — both prospective enrollees educating themselves and existing Settle In Peace customers tracking an active program.

3. MARKET GAP

Debt settlement is historically run by call centers — consumers get sold a program over the phone and then wait for status calls. Mainstream budgeting apps do not track settlement enrollment at all. Settle In Peace is built for that specific moment: it lets a consumer self-assess, learn what settlement actually involves, and — once enrolled — see trust-account progress and documents from their phone instead of calling an agent.

4. BETA TESTING

Internal testing only — no external beta cohort; we want to be precise about that. We iterated through internal builds on physical devices (builds 6 through 10 on this version) and applied findings directly:

- Account deletion was added in-app (Profile → Delete Account) backed by a deployed endpoint, after review of Guideline 5.1.1 requirements.
- The assessment flow was hardened so a partially completed questionnaire restores cleanly on relaunch.
- Auth edge cases (expired tokens, offline start) were fixed to degrade to the login screen rather than a spinner.

The reviewer path: sign in with the demo account in App Review Information — it lands on a populated dashboard with a completed assessment.

5. SUITE OF RELATED APPS

No — Settle In Peace is a standalone product. Our developer account (Reid Technology) hosts several independent products in unrelated industries; none share a product surface with Settle In Peace. It connects only to settleinpeace.com infrastructure.

6. COULD THIS BE A FEATURE OF ANOTHER APP ON THE ACCOUNT

No. The other apps on our account are independent businesses in unrelated categories (crypto trading, fleet management, farm community). A debt-settlement program tracker cannot be an in-app purchase or feature of any of them — different backend, different data model, different audience, and a regulated subject area that must stay isolated and clearly scoped.

7. SHARED CODE ON THE ACCOUNT

None beyond open-source frameworks. Within its own monorepo, the mobile app consumes our first-party typed SDK (@settle/shared-sdk — the same API client the settleinpeace.com web app uses). Nothing is shared with any other product on the developer account — the SDK is private to the Settle repository.

8. THIRD-PARTY SHARED CODE

No. Beyond standard open-source libraries — the Expo SDK / React Native framework, React Navigation, AsyncStorage / expo-secure-store, and a passkey library — the entire codebase is first-party code written and owned by Reid Technology. No commercialized template, white-label framework, or app-generation service was used.

9. CLIENT-PROVIDED APP OR CONTENT

No. Settle In Peace is a first-party product. Reid Technology owns the product, the brand, the codebase, and the platform it connects to (settleinpeace.com / api.settleinpeace.com). No client commissioned or provided the app, its design, or its content; it is not a reskin of any other product.
