# Settle In Peace — Visual Philosophy

## Movement: "Calm Arrival"

One idea, one line: **turbulence that resolves.** The mark is a single
waveform — agitated, erratic oscillation on the left that decays into a
perfectly calm horizon on the right. Debt on the left. Peace on the right.
No icons of coins, no scales, no shaking hands. The relief *is* the image.

## Color

- **Emerald (#10b981 → #34d399)** — resolution, growth after clearing. The
  calm half of the line is the brightest half: the destination glows.
- **Slate-950 (#020617)** — the depth the debt came out of. The mark floats
  above it, never drowns in it.
- The line itself gradients left→right: dim slate-teal where the noise is
  loud, luminous emerald where it goes quiet.

## Form

- One continuous stroke. Never broken — the journey is unbroken.
- The flat horizon extends slightly past the decay point: peace is not the
  absence of motion, it is a steady state that continues.
- A single small disc rests just above the settled horizon — a sun at rest.
  It is the only filled element in the composition; everything else is line.

## Typography

- "SETTLE" — semibold, tight. "IN PEACE" — light, wide letterspacing.
  The weight literally settles as you read.
- No tagline in the mark. The line already says it.

## Craft

Every curve is a true damped cosine, computed, not drawn by eye — the decay
envelope is exact. Nothing here is decoration. If an element could be
removed without losing meaning, it was.
